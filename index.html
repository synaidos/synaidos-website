Why more data doesn't close it

It's optics, not model quality.

A camera fixed in one position sees the shelf from a single optical centre. Whatever sits behind the front facing is hidden from that point, and no amount of training data recovers what was never in the image. A phone walked along the bay sees the same fixture from many positions. That baseline is geometry, not software.

01

One viewpoint

A fixed camera reports the front face well. Behind it, depth and capacity have to be guessed from what the front looks like.

02

Many viewpoints

A walked phone sees the bay from a moving baseline, so depth is observed rather than assumed. Positions it still cannot see stay marked unknown.

03

A complement, not a replacement

Fixed shelf vision keeps watching the front. ShelfSense measures the fixture and what is behind the facing, with no hardware on the shelf.

Trust is a product feature

ShelfSense is built to say “we don't know.”
